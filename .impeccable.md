# Design Context

## Users

Deepak, alone — a developer cleaning up his own 250GB MacBook, opened occasionally (not daily) when storage gets tight or out of curiosity after a scan. Secondary "user" is anyone he shows the tool to as a demo (a job application video walkthrough) — for them it's a 2-3 minute glance, not hands-on use. State of mind: focused, slightly impatient (wants the answer fast), and — during the Cleanup flow specifically — a little wary, since deletion is a real, irreversible-feeling action even though it goes to Trash.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, technical. This is a diagnostic tool, not a consumer app — it earns trust by being exact and legible, not by being friendly or playful. No mascot energy, no forced delight. The one place warmth is earned is confirmation and success states (a scan completing, space freed) — quiet acknowledgment, not celebration.

## Aesthetic Direction

Dark theme (matches the macOS Storage settings reference and fits focused, at-a-desk cleanup sessions). No named references given — direction is derived from purpose: a real systems/diagnostic tool, closer to an observability dashboard than a lifestyle app. Explicitly avoiding generic AI-dashboard tells: no cyan-on-dark/purple-gradient palette, no glassmorphism, no card-grid-of-icons layout, no gradient text, no left-border accent stripes anywhere (the treemap's category color-coding already does that job — a second accent-stripe system would be redundant and is exactly the AI-slop pattern to avoid).

Typography and color palette are chosen fresh per the impeccable font/color procedures at build time (T009/T010) — not fixed here, so as not to pre-empt that process with a lazy default.

## Design Principles

1. **Real numbers are the hero, not decoration around them.** The treemap itself — real proportions, real category colors — carries the interface; chrome around it stays quiet.
2. **Irreversible actions get maximum visual weight, everything else stays out of the way.** The delete confirmation (exact paths, exact total size) is the one moment allowed to demand full attention; the rest of the UI should never compete with it or with itself for urgency.
3. **Density over cards.** This is a data-dense diagnostic tool — resist wrapping every category/file/flag in its own card. Use typography, spacing rhythm, and the treemap's own geometry for hierarchy instead.
4. **AI Suggestions read as distinctly lower-certainty than rule-based flags** — a different visual register (not just a different color swatch) so the trust gradient is legible at a glance, not just in a tooltip.
5. **No motion for motion's sake.** One well-considered transition for state changes that matter (drilling into a folder, a scan completing) beats scattered micro-animations on a tool used in short, focused sessions.
